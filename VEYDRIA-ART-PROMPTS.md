## Prompt Engineering Notes

### For ChatGPT (DALL-E 3)
- **Strengths:** Responds well to cinematic framing, aspect ratio hints, specific artist references, and mood keywords
- **Technique:** Start with the broad scene type ("A sweeping matte painting of..."), then layer in specific details. End with style references and lighting direction
- **Avoid:** Overloading with more than ~5-6 specific visual elements per prompt; DALL-E 3 starts dropping details around that threshold

### For Gemini (Image Generation)
- **Strengths:** Handles dense descriptive text well, understands symbolic and cultural references, produces richer texture detail
- **Technique:** Front-load with place names and cultural context, use dense descriptive prose rather than list format, include emotional atmosphere
- **Avoid:** Vague style references; Gemini prefers specific descriptive language over abstract style names

### Universal Tips
- Both models benefit from **real-world analogs** ("Bhutanese dzong," "Saharan erg," "Khmer baray") — they ground the generation in actual visual references
- **Lighting direction** is the single biggest quality differentiator — always specify time of day and light quality
- **Color palette** notes help maintain consistency across a series of generations
- For **character portraits**, always specify age, expression, and one distinctive physical trait — this prevents generic fantasy faces
